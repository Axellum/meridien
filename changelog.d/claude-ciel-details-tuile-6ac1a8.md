### Changed

- **The *Details* page's sky is a 64-column tile instead of a full-screen image**: like the main screen (PR 27), the page now lets LVGL repeat a 64 × height tile of the dithered sky (`bg_image_tiled`) instead of keeping a 1.2 to 1.8 MB image in PSRAM, and no longer draws its own gradient under it. Same pixels (180 of 180 PC-bench renders of the scene `details` identical at 50, 28 and 25 lines); about 1.1 to 1.75 MB of PSRAM less while the page is open (from the image size, not measured). Not run on a board and the page's opening time has not been measured: *not tested on board*. Details: [docs/mesures/details-ciel-en-mosaique.md](../docs/mesures/details-ciel-en-mosaique.md). *(Français : le ciel de la page « Détails » est une tuile de 64 colonnes que LVGL répète, comme sur l'accueil ; mêmes pixels au banc du PC, non essayé sur carte.)*
