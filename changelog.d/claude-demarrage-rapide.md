### Changed

- **Faster start-up, first light earlier**: the PSRAM memory test at boot is turned off (`CONFIG_SPIRAM_MEMTEST=n`; it took 885 ms) and the fixed 100 ms wait before the backlight is replaced by the real end of the last display transfer plus one panel scan period (20 ms). On the Waveshare 7B the light comes on **1030 ms after the start of the bootloader instead of 1996.5 ms** (mean of 6 restarts each, read on the serial log; [measurement](../docs/mesures/demarrage-7b.md)). The boot log line "rétroéclairage allumé" now also gives the time spent waiting. The memory is no longer swept at every start. *Démarrage plus rapide : test de la PSRAM coupé et attente fixe de 100 ms remplacée par la fin réelle du dernier transfert ; la lumière s'allume à 1 030 ms au lieu de 1 996 ms sur la 7B.* Tested on: `waveshare-7b`, 2026-11-09, commit `86d5352` (timing read on the log; image on the panel judged clean by the author's eye; Tab5 not tested on board).
