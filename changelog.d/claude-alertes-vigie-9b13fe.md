### Added

- **Official weather alerts from Vigie** (the author's own alert service): the main screen shows a pill in the top bar **only while an alert is in force** (no permanent banner), coloured by level in every theme, with the issuer's own title; a tap opens a page listing every alert in force (phenomenon, level, period, the issuer's text, who relayed it: Vigie and its sources) and always says that this is **not** an official alert service. The issuer's words are never rewritten: a text the font cannot draw is dropped whole and the next language is tried. Seven languages (German, Dutch, Spanish, Italian and Turkish written by the agent, **not proofread**). Decision and numbers: [ADR-0009](../docs/decisions/0009-alertes-officielles-par-vigie.md), [docs/mesures/alertes.md](../docs/mesures/alertes.md). Tested on: PC only (pure-core host tests with AddressSanitizer and UBSan, mutation checks, ten real Vigie answers captured on 2026-11-07, PC renders); compiled for the Tab5 and the Waveshare 7B; **not tested on a board** (no flash, nothing looked at on the panel, no real alert received, TLS cost on the board not measured).
- **Four alert settings** on the screen (page « Lieu et météo » → « Alertes »), on the phone page (card « Official alerts ») and in the API (`GET`/`PUT /api/alerts`, [docs/api-reglages.md](../docs/api-reglages.md) section 14): on/off (on by default), Vigie address (empty = the public instance; otherwise `https://` is required), refresh period (10 to 180 minutes, 15 by default, one request per period, no burst at start-up, back-off after an error), minimum level (yellow by default). Tested on: PC only (host tests and mutations of the registry, the host of the device and the API); not tested on a board.

### Fixed

- **Alerts: no more one-second silence at the change of the hour.** Two alerts that follow each other, the first ending at `hh:59:59` (AEMET) and the second starting at `hh:00:00` (DWD), left the screen without an alert for one second (the pill could disappear and come back). An end written at `hh:59:59` now counts as the last second of the alert; any other end stays exclusive. PC tests only, not seen on a board.
