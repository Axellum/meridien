### Added

- **Sunrise and sunset computed for the place** (NOAA method, in the pure core, no `<cmath>`): until the weather is read, the sky
  (day or night) is judged from the sunrise and sunset of the saved place and its local date instead of a fixed "8 h to 18 h"
  that was wrong everywhere and all year. A known weather answer keeps its own sunrise, sunset and day/night flag. Against
  Open-Meteo's real sunrise and sunset over 63 days (nine captured answers) the largest gap is 8 s.

### Changed

- **The time on screen is the time of the chosen place** ([ADR-0012](../docs/decisions/0012-l-heure-affichee-est-celle-du-lieu-choisi.md)):
  the clock, the date and the change of day, the label of each "hour by hour" column, sunrise and sunset, the day names, "today"
  and the start and end of an official alert now follow the time zone of the saved zone, read again on every call. Choosing Tokyo
  on the screen or on the phone page changes them at the next pass of the screen (500 ms), with nothing to restart. Nothing is
  written for Paris any more (Paris is only what the factory zone, Lyon, says): a zone whose time zone is not in the table shows
  no time ("-- : --" and the waiting sentence) instead of a wrong one. A column that is not on the hour reads "05:30" (an hour on
  the hour keeps "7h" in French, "7:00" in the other languages). The city name is now refreshed on every pass of the screen
  instead of every 30 s.

### Fixed

- **"Hour by hour" columns across a clock change**: each column is converted with the rules in force at its own instant, not with
  one offset for the whole window (New York on 1 November 2026 shows "1h" twice, Sydney on 4 October 2026 has no "2h"); the day
  names are read at the local noon of the forecast day and "today" is compared by local calendar day.
- **Known limit**: `Africa/Casablanca` is one hour off during Ramadan and from 20 September 2026 (the time zone table has a fixed
  `+01`; tzdata 2026c has `+00`), kept as a known limit in the tests. Details and numbers: [docs/mesures/fuseaux.md](../docs/mesures/fuseaux.md).

Tested on: PC only (38 new core tests run with AddressSanitizer and UBSan against expected values generated by Python `zoneinfo`
(tzdata 2026c), the real `main/horloge.cpp` run on the PC with a fake zone, 38 + 14 targeted mutations all detected, 7 PC renders
at 1024x600 looked at). Compiled on 07/11/2026 for the Tab5 (2 912 272 bytes) and for the Waveshare 7B (2 892 896 bytes), exit
code 0 and no compiler warning; **not flashed and not run on any board**: what the panel shows with another zone, a zone change
made from the phone page and the cost of the sunrise computation on the ESP32-P4 are not verified.
