### Added

- **Press-and-hold wheel on the home screen** (ADR-0021): a long press opens a wheel **at the finger** (the hub under it, buttons on a
  ring, a word under each button, the compass at the centre saying "Go to"). The first ring holds only **Settings**, **Details** (the page of
  the seven weather quantities, ADR-0020) and, when the alerts page exists, **Alerts**; touching a button opens its page, touching anywhere else or waiting 15 s closes the wheel, a
  slide does nothing. The positions and touch zones come from the pure geometry of ADR-0017; the wheel is **one LVGL object**
  drawn in a single event, created on opening and deleted on closing, with no animation. A second ring for a family of choices
  is ready (no family exists yet); each entry is one line in `composer_entrees`.
- **Pure core `ecran/roue_commande.hpp`**: the wheel's scale from the screen size (7B 0.8, Tab5 1.0), the slide threshold, the
  request made to the geometry, and what a touch does (choose, unfold, fold, close, nothing). Tested on the PC (compiled and
  run with AddressSanitizer and UBSan) with mutation tests. Tested on: not tested on board.
- **Icon font subset** `main/assets/mdi_roue.ttf` (4 glyphs of Material Design Icons, Apache-2.0, 1 380 bytes) generated by
  `tools/icones/generer_police_roue.py` from `tools/icones/roue_glyphes.json`; licence text and attribution in `THIRD_PARTY.md`.
- Render bench scene `roue` (`.\tools\rendre.ps1 -Scene roue`): the wheel at the centre, near a corner, near an edge, with and
  without a second ring, on 1280x720 and 1024x600.

### Changed

- **Settings and diagnostic are reached through the wheel** (long press, then "Settings"). The "Diagnostic" screen, which used to
  open on a long press, is now a row in Settings > Device. If the wheel cannot be built (font, memory), the long press opens the
  Settings directly.

### Removed

- The round **Settings button** at the top right of the home screen (one LVGL object fewer: 10 to 9); its place goes back to the
  place name, the data-state chip and the clock. Seen on: PC renders only; not tested on board.
