### Fixed

- **Waveshare 7B: the firmware no longer crashes at every boot.** The DSI bus asked for the PLL_F20M clock source, reserved to ESP32-P4 chips older than v3.0, while the 7B build requires v3.1 or newer: the driver called `abort()` before anything was drawn (11 boots out of 11). The source is now left to the driver (`bus_cfg.phy_clk_src = 0`). The Tab5 is untouched. Tested on: `waveshare-7b`, 2026-11-07, `9c89ad5` + `eac14d2`, serial log only: all milestones reached (screen, touch controller, codecs, “Carte prête” at 1.85 s, C6 link, Wi-Fi, IP address, time), 10 resets out of 10 without an error; **the picture on the panel and a real finger were not checked**. Details and limits: [docs/mesures/7b-materiel.md](../docs/mesures/7b-materiel.md).

### Changed

- **`tools/essai_7b.py`: backup limited to what can be read.** Over the ESP32-P4's USB-Serial/JTAG a 256-byte block of the running application never reads (`Packet content transfer stopped`, with or without the stub), so a full copy of the flash is impossible there. With `--reference <file>` the script keeps and reads twice `0x0`–`0x10000` and the old NVS, replaces the running application by its reference file, and the chip itself proves (`verify-flash`, MD5) that “start read + file” equals the flash; it refuses any trial that would touch another partition of the old firmware; `otadata` is no longer checked after the restart (the new firmware writes its state there). Tested on: `waveshare-7b`, 2026-11-07: backup, flash and restoration proved by the chip, ESPHome back (same MAC, version, 74 entities, no setting changed); self-test: 9 more checks.
