### Added

- **New main screen**: very large time and date in the language of the screen; weather now (big icon, temperature, feels-like, label, minimum and maximum); wind with a direction arrow and gusts; rain (probability, « rain in N min » when known); humidity; pressure and its trend; UV index in five levels; sunrise and sunset on an arc with the sun's position; moon phase (computed on the device); an hourly strip; seven days with a minimum-maximum bar; alerts (frost, heat, strong wind, heavy rain, storm); a sky background that follows the time of the day and the weather, dithered to avoid colour bands. Values the device does not receive yet (hourly strip, pressure trend, rain amount) are hidden, never invented. Works at 1280 × 720, 720 × 1280 and 1024 × 600, in the seven languages. Design, criteria and choice: [docs/design/ecran-principal.md](../docs/design/ecran-principal.md); decision: [ADR-0008](../docs/decisions/0008-ecran-principal-par-zones-themes-et-ciel-trame.md). Tested on: Tab5 ST7121 on 2026-11-07 (commit `cd362eb`), serial log only, panel not looked at: the screen is built and drawn, no crash over 156 s **with the memory settings of jalon 1b** (without them the board reboots about 14 s after start, see [docs/depannage.md](../docs/depannage.md)); compiled for the ESP32-P4 (2,722,192 bytes); rendered on the PC in French, German and Turkish; pure core: 64 tests, 42 mutations detected out of 42.
- **Original weather icon set**: drawn by `tools/icones/generer_icones.py`, kept in flash (825,032 bytes, no RAM for the pixels), no third-party artwork. Tested on: Tab5 ST7121 on 2026-11-07, log only (built without error); icons not looked at on the panel.
- **Four themes**: *Ciel* (default), *Tableau sombre*, *Horloge*, *Clair*; the existing « thème » setting (auto, clair, sombre) picks *Ciel*, *Clair* or *Tableau sombre* and the switch is live. *Horloge* is not reachable from that setting yet. Tested on: the switch is checked on the PC (10 pairs identical pixel for pixel); not tested on a board.

### Changed

- **Render bench**: thirteen variants of the main screen, a themes scene, a live theme-switch check (`tools/rendu/verifier_bascule.py`) and `BANC_CHRONO=1` for relative timings on the PC ([docs/outils.md](../docs/outils.md)).
- **Main screen**: starts the guided installation again at boot when it was not finished (`ui_installation_ouvrir_si_necessaire()`, asked by the settings session; a stub does nothing until that session's code is merged). Tested on: compiled only.
